Integrity

Every byte is checked against its SHA-256 at each step: when written, whenever read, and during background scrubs.

  1. WriteNode rejects any blob whose bytes don't match the checksum sent with it.
  2. StoreChecksum is kept in the blob header on disk; writes are atomic.
  3. ReadNode re-verifies before serving; the gateway verifies again after transfer.
  4. ScrubBackground passes re-verify everything at rest; bad copies are quarantined and rebuilt.
